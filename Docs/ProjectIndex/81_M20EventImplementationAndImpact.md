configurations return **Review required**: passing alternatives exist, but supported

candidate measures IL −22.999897779673 LUFS, TP −1.585269634075 dBTP and LRA
17.373082370452 LU against the explicitly selected 18 LU requirement. Its coverage
remains **known partial + uncertain**, and it is not automatically recommended.

The detector associates a tentative entrance bracket at 10.05–10.16 s with continuing
activity through the song. It does **not** establish the user's approximate 12.283 s
musical entrance. Increasing the duration allowance to 60% does not resolve that
uncertainty. The report preserves the evidence; no file-specific retuning or claim
of musical correctness is made.

assessments/64 proxies and find no acceptable candidate. The cinema retained result
has LRA about 23.562825 LU against 15 LU and TP about −0.815701 dBTP against −1 dBTP.
